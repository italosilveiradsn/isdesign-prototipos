Case study · WordPress

From static design to an editable theme.

Agencies and clients need to change their own text, photos and colors. I turned the Casa Roofing concept into a WordPress theme that keeps the exact look and gives the client a visual editor.

  • Pixel match with the original design: 0.0% visual difference on desktop and mobile in my comparison test.
  • 91 fields in Appearance > Customize (copy, photos and contact data) plus six brand colors, with live preview.
  • Working contact form with spam protection. Lighthouse mobile: 99 performance, 100 accessibility, 96 best practices.
  • Not yet tested on a production host with real email delivery, and it is a one-page theme.
