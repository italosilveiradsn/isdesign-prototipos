Feature § 02

A family business, wired from day one.

Father
to son.

Howard’s Electrical Service Co. is a local, family-owned business that understands all electrical systems, and the love someone has for their business, family and profession.

Electrical contractor Rob Bentz grew up listening to his father passionately talk about all the projects he was working on. Eventually, a spark of interest presented itself and Rob’s knowledge began to grow.

Today, Rob owns the business his father once started and strives to uphold the reputation the company has gained.
