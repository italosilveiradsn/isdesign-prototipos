What we do.

01 — RESIDENTIAL

Residential roofing

Vantage Roofing's Austin-based residential roofing services are top notch and highly recommended by Austin residents. Whether you need repairs, replacements, installations, or maintenance on existing roofs, our team has the expertise and know-how to get the job done quickly and efficiently. Our work is backed by a 10-year warranty for newly installed materials and labor, and we clean up after the job is done, leaving your home neat and tidy.

02 — COMMERCIAL

Commercial roofing

Austin-based Vantage Roofing specializes in commercial roofing services, new construction or reroofs. We provide Austin companies with affordable and reliable commercial roofing options. Our TPO roofs are known for their energy efficiency, while our metal roofs are renowned as an economical solution which offers superior protection against damage from the hot Texas sun and other elements.
