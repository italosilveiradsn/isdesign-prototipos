How it works

Guiding you step by step

STEP 1

CONSULTATION

Tell us about your project and goals by calling us or completing our online form to schedule your free, no obligation inspection.

STEP 2

PROPOSAL

Once we've gathered all the details on what the project entails and how it will be done, we will present our proposal including plans, timelines, and costs for approval. Have insurance? We can provide your insurance provider with our estimate and scope of work.

STEP 3

INSTALLATION

While we work on repairing or replacing your roof, our team is committed to maintaining a clutter-free, professional work site.

STEP 4

FINAL INSPECTION

Once the job is completed, we will perform a full inspection to ensure the safety of your family or staff. If an issue comes up at any time with the work we've done, we will fix it at no cost to you, regardless of how long ago it was completed.
